About

I care about what happens after the plan meets the course.

A course can have the right objectives and still be hard to teach. A project plan can be accurate and still leave faculty, designers, or students without what they need. Most of my work has been in that space: managing instructional design projects, coordinating course development, supporting online and hybrid delivery, and keeping accessibility and quality from becoming afterthoughts.

What I tend to work on

  • Online and hybrid course development, review, and readiness workflows.
  • Competency alignment, outcomes, objectives, and proficiency-based education planning.
  • Faculty support, designer guidance, quality review, and accessibility expectations.
  • Dashboards, trackers, and documentation for work that otherwise depends on memory.

How I work

I prefer clear systems, low-friction tools, and documentation that serves the people who actually have to use it. My role is often to help the right people stay in the same conversation long enough to make a course better: faculty, designers, program leaders, students, and the people responsible for delivery. The work can be messy. That is not a failure. It is usually where the real requirements start to show up.
