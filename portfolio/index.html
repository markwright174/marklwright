Portfolio

Work worth explaining.

This is a place to collect examples of the work I can share publicly. Some are finished learning objects. Some are tools, prototypes, or support materials. What matters to me is the problem behind each piece and how the work helped make a course, process, or decision easier to manage.

This is a selected set of examples, not a complete record of my work. Much of my recent work involves managing instructional design projects, supervising team processes, coordinating with faculty and stakeholders, and supporting proprietary course-development work that cannot be shared publicly. The examples here are meant to show the kinds of problems I work on, the tools and structures I build, and how I think about learning work in practice.
