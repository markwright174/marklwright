Volunteer Orientation Pathfinder.

A short Storyline prototype for a familiar onboarding problem: someone wants to help, but the path into the work is not yet clear.

Open prototype Back to category
Open preview in a new tab ↗

Independent prototype based on public HRC Houston information. It is not official HRC training and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HRC.

What was happening

New volunteers may need help seeing where their interests fit before choosing a role.

What I built

I built a guided choice activity that explains volunteer paths and suggests a starting point from low-stakes fit questions.

What it demonstrates

Articulate Storyline Volunteer onboarding Guided choice Self-assessment Accessibility-aware interaction AI-assisted prototyping

My role

I analyzed public information, designed and built the Storyline interaction, and completed a first-pass accessibility review.
