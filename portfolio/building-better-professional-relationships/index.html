Learning object

Building Better Professional Relationships.

An Articulate Rise course about treating professional relationships as a practical skill: mapping the people around the work, understanding mutual value, and maintaining useful connections over time.

This is a portfolio learning object built in Articulate Rise. It uses AI-assisted drafting and activity generation, with human review and shaping around the learning goal.

Open course Back to category

What was happening

Professional relationships often get treated as background noise: useful when they happen, awkward when they do not, and rarely something people are taught to manage with intention. The course reframes that work as a practical habit rather than a personality test.

What I built

I built a six-lesson Rise experience that moves from why relationship management matters into concrete practice: mapping a network, recognizing the difference between networking and relationship-building, finding mutual value, maintaining contact over time, and leaving with a personal plan.

What it demonstrates

Articulate Rise Professional skills Scenario-based learning Relationship management Reflection planning AI-assisted course drafting

My role

I shaped the learning flow, reviewed and revised the AI-assisted draft material, organized the Rise build, and kept the experience pointed toward a usable workplace outcome. The goal was not to make a dense theory course. It was to give learners a small set of habits they could actually carry into their next professional conversation.
